Experience

MMHF

I had the opportunity to work for MMHF, which is an e-commerce business. For my job, I had to assess the value of lots we were looking to buy. We would only bid if the lots had 5x the value of what we were paying for them.

We would then figure out which parts we bought and determine how to price them. This gave me the opportunity to work with products from numerous industries, ranging from health care to aerospace.
