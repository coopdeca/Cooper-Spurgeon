Sign Up for a Session

Complete the registration form below to sign up for the Cooper Spurgeon Track & Field Camp.

Sessions are $40 each, and athletes can attend as many sessions as they would like.

Before You Register

  • Choose the session or sessions you would like to attend.
  • Each group is limited to 6 athletes.
  • Only move up to a higher age group if you feel your child is ready.
  • Bring water and snacks to each session.
  • Bring spikes if they are needed for your event.
