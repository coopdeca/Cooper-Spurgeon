Learn. Train. Compete.

As a former Iowa State decathlete, I had the opportunity to compete in all 10 events of the decathlon and learn what it takes to develop as a complete track and field athlete.

I am excited to pass along what I have learned throughout my athletic career and help athletes build a strong foundation in their events. Whether you're just getting started or looking to take your skills to the next level, my goal is to help athletes become more confident, knowledgeable, and prepared to compete.

I look forward to working with all of the athletes and helping them get ready for their upcoming middle school and high school seasons.
